# MoonMuse Studio — The Celestial Collection

**Positioning:** Apple meets astronomy. Minimal, luxurious, elegant. Soft cream,
midnight navy, warm gold, white, lavender accents, whitespace as a feature,
rounded corners, serif display type.

**Design decisions that hold everything together**
1. **One motif library.** Every product draws from the same SVG vocabulary —
   the 4-point gold sparkle (the signature mark), thin-stroke moon phases,
   dashed constellations, dot-star fields, soft vertical gradients. Nothing is
   decorated twice in two different styles.
2. **Two grounds only.** Paper (cream/white, for daytime products: planner,
   journal, cards, light wallpapers) and midnight navy (covers, dark
   wallpapers, dividers). Gold is *light*, used at hairline weight — never a
   paint bucket.
3. **Typography does the luxury.** Serif display (Georgia in the files;
   Playfair Display is the free commercial-safe substitute for editables) +
   letterspaced sans caps for labels. No bold headlines anywhere.
4. **Original vector art throughout** — generated in-house, zero stock, zero
   third-party assets. 100% commercial-use safe; nothing needs replacement
   before sale.
5. **Undated and reusable.** Planner and journal are undated so the products
   never expire and never generate "wrong year" refunds.

## Honest notes (kept in customer-facing READMEs too)
- **Canva kit:** templates ship as pixel-perfect PNG layouts at native platform
  sizes with a step-by-step "import as background → add your text in Canva"
  guide. They are not .canva files (那 requires publishing through Canva's own
  template program). Identified clearly per the brief.
- **Notion kit:** ships as a complete build document + icon set, because Notion
  templates only exist inside Notion. Once built (≈20 min), the store owner
  shares their own duplication link — that link is what customers buy.
- **Fonts:** the PDFs embed Georgia/DejaVu (safe to sell as PDFs). For editable
  derivatives, swap to Playfair Display + Inter (SIL OFL).
