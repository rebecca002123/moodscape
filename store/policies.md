# Store policies — Ember & Constella (9rcuf8-yp.myshopify.com)

Drafted for a UK-based store selling **digital downloads only**. The Shopify API
token in this session lacks the `write_legal_policies` scope, so paste these in
yourself: **Shopify admin → Settings → Policies**. (The default Shopify privacy
policy already installed is a good template that auto-fills your store details —
keep it.)

> These drafts are a starting point, not legal advice — have them reviewed
> before you rely on them.

## Also: store name

The Admin API cannot rename a store. To change "My Store 5":
**Settings → General → Store details → Edit → Store name** → suggest
**"Ember & Constella"**.

## Refund policy (paste into "Return and refund policy")

Everything in this store is a digital download (wallpapers, printable journals
and art prints). Nothing is shipped, so there is nothing to return in the post.

**Instant access and the 14-day cooling-off period.** If you are a consumer in
the UK or EU, you normally have a 14-day right to cancel an online purchase.
For digital content, that right ends once the download begins with your express
consent. By completing checkout and starting your download, you consent to
immediate delivery of the digital content and acknowledge that you lose the
statutory right to cancel once the download has begun.

**When we will refund you:**

- **Faulty or corrupted files** — if a file will not open, is incomplete, or
  does not match its product description, contact us within 30 days and we will
  replace it or refund you in full. Under the Consumer Rights Act 2015, digital
  content must be as described, fit for purpose and of satisfactory quality.
- **Duplicate purchases** — accidentally bought the same product twice? We will
  refund the duplicate.
- **Not yet downloaded** — if you have not downloaded anything and contact us
  within 14 days of purchase, we will refund you.

**How to ask.** Email us via the contact details on this store with your order
number. We aim to reply within 2 working days, and approved refunds are
returned to your original payment method within 10 working days.
